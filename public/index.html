ℹ️ Model Methodology & Formulas
1. IMD Heat Index (HI):
HI = T + 0.55 × (1 - 0.01 × RH) × (T - 14.5)
Where T = Air Temp (°C) and RH = Relative Humidity (%)
2. Vulnerability Score (V):
V = (0.5 × LST_norm) + (0.5 × (1 - NDVI_norm))
Weighted combination of normalized Land Surface Temp & vegetation deficit.
3. Overall Risk Matrix:
Risk = Hazard Class × Vulnerability Score
Disclaimer: Model estimates are generated using satellite thermal observations and empirical atmospheric equations for micro-climate screening.
