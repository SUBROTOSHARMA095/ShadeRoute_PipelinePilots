📖 Environmental Metrics & Indices Plain English Guide
🚶 Pedestrian Exposure LST × (1 - NDVI)
Walking heat stress combining hot unshaded ground with lack of tree canopy. Rated High, Medium, or Low.
🌡️ Ground Heat LST (technical name)
How hot the actual ground surface is in °C — measured via high-resolution thermal infrared sensors.
🌿 Green Canopy Score NDVI (technical name)
How much healthy, cooling plant cover exists. High = thick shade canopy. Low = bare exposed ground.
🏗️ Concrete & Rooftops NDBI (technical name)
Buildings, roads, and concrete surfaces that absorb heat during the day and release it at night.
🌱 Bare Soil Exposure BSI (technical name)
Unplanted dry soil patches baking in direct sunlight — the hottest zones on campus.
💧 Moisture Level NDWI (technical name)
How much water is present in plants and soil. Moist areas stay cooler.
🌳 Tree Cover % Veg% (technical name)
The percentage of that ground patch that's actually covered by leaves and shade.

🛰️ Behind the Scenes

How ShadeRoute Works

Three simple steps — from high-resolution climate observation to your campus plan in seconds.

🛰️ High-Resolution Environmental Monitoring
Landsat 9 and Sentinel-5 earth observation data measure ground surface temperature, atmospheric conditions, tree canopy cover, and built density for every 10-metre sector of the campus and neighbourhood.
🧠 AI Finds the Worst Heat Traps
A machine learning model evaluates which open walkways and plazas are most dangerous — combining heat level, how many students walk there, and whether there's any existing shade — then scores each zone from 0 to 100.
📍 You Decide Where to Cool
Use the sliders to choose how many shade trees or mist sprayers you want to add. ShadeRoute instantly pins the exact optimal locations on the map — so you can see your Campus Action Plan come to life.
🧮 Mathematical Modeling

Scientific Models & Score Formulas

Exact analytical formulas, peer-reviewed literature, and institutional criteria powering every score across ShadeRoute:

🚶 Pedestrian Exposure LST_norm × (1 - NDVI_norm)
Physical outdoor walking heat stress rated into High, Medium, and Low terciles. High ground surface temperature combined with a lack of tree canopy shade yields the highest exposure.
Primary Citations:
⚠️ Heat Stress Index (HHSI) HI_IMD × [1 + (0.30 × VI)]
Scales the base IMD heat index by up to +30% based on local vulnerability. Dense, unshaded sectors with higher vulnerable populations receive the greatest risk amplification.
Primary Citations:
👥 Vulnerability Index (VI) 45.5% Pop + 36.4% Fac + 18.1% Veg
Composite vulnerability combining age-vulnerable population density (children & seniors), proximity to schools and hospitals, and vegetative canopy deficit across the 3 km² study zone.
Primary Citation:
🌡️ Feels-Like Heat (HI_IMD) Rothfusz(Air Temp, RH)
Standard meteorological apparent heat index calculating human physiological heat strain from ambient air temperature and relative humidity.
Primary Citations:
🎯 Intervention Priority (0–100) Multi-Criteria Urgency
Ranks campus cooling hotspots for the Action Plan using peak ground heat (Landsat 9), concrete density (NDBI), shade deficiency (NDVI), and pedestrian walkways.
Primary Citations:
🏥 Hospital Patient Surge Model Y_t = Y_0 × [1 + (P_hw × β_alert × C_lag)]
Predicts casualty admissions and outpatient footfall during heatwave days for study area facilities (Student Health Centre, Jagamara UPHC, Astang Ayurveda, and IMS & SUM Hospital). Quantifies syndromic cases (sunstroke, cardiovascular, AKI) and readiness needs (HSU beds, IV fluids).
